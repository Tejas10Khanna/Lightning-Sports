date: 2021-06-22

---

On 22nd June 2021 around 12:20 PM, The Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) released a statement on Pakistan Men's Team Batting Coach Younis Khan.

PCB says that Younis Khan and the PCB have agreed to seperate ways. Younis Khan was appointed in November 2020 as the batting coach o the Pakistan Men's Cricket team on a two- year contract that was scheduled to run till the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2022.

PCB Chief Executive Wasim Khan says “It is sad to lose an expert of the stature and experience of Younis Khan. Following a series of discussions, we both have reluctantly but mutually and amicably agreed that it was time to move in different directions.  
  
“I want to thank Younis Khan for his contributions during his short stint as the Pakistan men’s national cricket team’s batting coach and hope he will remain available to assist the PCB by sharing his vast knowledge with the emerging cricketers.”

Pakistan Men's Cricket team will have to tour England for a ODI and T20 series without a batting coach, But Younis Khan's replacement for the batting coach of the team will be appointed before the team's West Indies tour.

Pakistan men’s national cricket team will tour England from 25 June to 20 July for three ODIs and three T20Is and the West Indies tour will be from 21 July to 24 August where they will have five T20Is and two Tests.
