Warne’s management said that he passed away in Thailand, of a suspected heart attack.  
  
Their statement read “Shane was found unresponsive in his villa and despite the best efforts of medical staff, he could not be revived.”

Warne's demise has given a big blow to the whole world of cricket, especially after the demise of another iconic cricketer Rod Marsh who died on Friday.

Shane Warne had played 145 tests and 194 ODIs for Australia. He was named as one of the bowler in Australia's 'greatest ever ODI team'. Warne was inducted into ICC Cricket Hall of Fame in 2013.
