**Earlier condition of Ligue 1 broadcasting in India.**

The main Indian broadcasters had not shown any interest in LIGUE 1, but with the Leo Messi deal with PSG, the fan rating of Ligue 1 has also spiked in India.

The Broadcasters would now be looking forward to getting the Broadcasting rights of Ligue 1 for India.

For now there are no major channels in India who broadcast the Ligeu 1.

**How to watch LIGUE 1 in India:**

Other than major channels to there is a channel on which the Broadcast of Ligue 1 will be aired, but the major difficulty is that the channel only broadcasts one match of Ligue 1 per week. **TVH5Monde Asie** will be airing Ligue 1 in India. The third match of the PSG club and probably the first match of Leo Messi for PSG against Brest is set to be aired on the channel on 21st August.

The channel can be viewed in major DTH providers like Tata Sky and Airtel Digital TV India, and can also be streamed on Jio TV app.
