The Selection Committee of the BCCI on Wednesday, named 18-member squads each for ODI and T20I series against West Indies starting from 6th February and consisting of 3 ODIs and 3 T20Is.

Kuldeep Yadav

* * *

Jasprit Bumrah and Mohd. Shami have been given rest for the whole series. KL Rahul will join the team in 2nd ODI.

**Key Points** :

Ravi Bishnoi has been included in both the squads and Kuldeep Yadav has a comeback in the Indian ODI team after the Sri Lanka series.

According to the BCCI, Ravindra Jadeja is still not completely recovered from the knee injury, and will not be a part of the series.
