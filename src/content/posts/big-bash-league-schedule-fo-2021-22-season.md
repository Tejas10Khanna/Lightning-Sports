The Big-Bash League schedule has been announced by the Australian Cricket Board previously, the season will kickoff from 5th of December and will be having 56 league matches in almost one and a half month. The Playoff will have Eliminator, Qualifier, Knock Out, Challenger and Final. Unlike 2020/21 season the Australian Cricket Board has decided to have all the matches with proper home and away scheduling, which was not in the past season due to Covid-19. The BigBash league will have a clash with the Ashes, which will be starting from 8th of September. So, it can result to the unavailability of players who will play the test matches against England.

The full Big-Bash League schedule can be seen below:
