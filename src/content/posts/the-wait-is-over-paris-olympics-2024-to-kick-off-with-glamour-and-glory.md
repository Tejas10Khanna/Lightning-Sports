The Paris Olympics 2024 are all set to begin with an exuberant opening ceremony on 26th of July @ 9:30 PM (IST)

This will mark the third time Paris has hosted the Summer Olympics, after previously hosting in 1900 and 1924. The 2024 Olympics are expected to feature approximately 10,000 athletes from around the world, competing in 32 sports and 5 new disciplines, including breakdancing, surfing, skateboarding, sport climbing, and karate. The games will be held at various venues across Paris and its suburbs, with the iconic Eiffel Tower serving as a backdrop for several events. The Paris Olympics promise to be an exciting and memorable event, showcasing the world's top athletes and promoting unity and sportsmanship.

## **Indian Contingent at Paris Olympics 2024**

  
  
The Indian contingent for the 2024 Paris Olympics consists of 117 athletes (70 men and 47 women) competing in 16 sports disciplines.
