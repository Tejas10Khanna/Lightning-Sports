UEFA Champions League 2021/22 is returning from 14th of September. FC Bayern Munich will be coming as defending champions with Manuel Neuer as it's captain.

Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo who just made their transfers from their previous teams respectively would be the center of the eyes. Lio Messi came to Ligue 1 club _Paris-Saint German_ from FC Barcelona. Cristiano Ronaldo just came to his old club _Manchester United_ from Italian club Juventus.

The details about the groups and teams can be seen here: [UEFA Champions League 2021/22 Group details 👇](https://lightningsportsin.wordpress.com/2021/09/14/uefa-champions-league-2021-22-group-details-%f0%9f%91%87/)

The viewers in India can see this edition of UEFA Champions League on Sony Sports Network. On channels Sony Ten 2 and Sony Six. Viewers can stream the matches on the SonyLiv app. Jio users can view the matches on Jio TV app.
