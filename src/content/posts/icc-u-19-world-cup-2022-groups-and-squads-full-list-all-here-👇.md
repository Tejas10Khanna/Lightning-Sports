The 14th U-19 World Cup will be held in four nations in the Caribbean, with 48 matches in total. The group stage will be held from January 14 to 22, with the plate stage beginning from the 25th January. The final will be held on February 5 in Sir Vivian Richards Stadium, North Sound, West Indies .India are the most successful team having won four titles in 2000, 2008, 2012 and 2018 and being runner-up twice in 2016 and 2020.

**ICC U-19 World Cup 2022 Groups:**  
  
**Group A** – Bangladesh, England, Canada, UAE  
  
**Group B** – India, Ireland, South Africa, Uganda  
  
**Group C** – Afghanistan, Pakistan, PNG, Zimbabwe  
  
**Group D** – Australia, Scotland, Sri Lanka, West Indies
