Indian and South African teams are ready for the 2nd test match of the Freedom series at the Wanderers Stadium, Johannesburg.

Indian captain Virat Kohli misses out due to an upper back spasm, Hanuma Vihari replaces him. KL Rahul will be the standing Captain of the team, with Jaspreet Bumrah being the Vice-captain.

Shardul Thakur  
Ravichandran Ashwin

Japrit Bumrah (Vice-Captain)
