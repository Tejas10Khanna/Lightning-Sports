The ICC T20 World Cup is set to start from 17th of October, hence on Tuesday, ICC announced the fixtures for the World Cup which is set to happen on UAE. Read about [ICC confirms that the ICC Twenty20 World Cup 2021 to be shifted to UAE and Oman](https://lightningsportsin.wordpress.com/2021/06/29/icc-confirms-that-the-icc-twenty20-world-cup-2021-to-be-shifted-to-uae-and-oman/)

The World Cup draws had been announced by the ICC previously, Read about it: [ICC T20 World Cup draws announced.](https://lightningsportsin.wordpress.com/2021/07/16/icc-t20-world-cup-draws-announced/)

The World Cup will start on 17th of October with Oman vs Papua New Guinea and Bangladesh vs Scotland which will be played in Oman.

The detailed schedule below:

17 October, Oman vs Papua New Guinea, Oman  
17 October, Bangladesh vs Scotland, Oman  
  
18 October, Ireland vs Netherlands, Abu Dhabi, UAE  
18 October, Sri Lanka vs Namibia, Abu Dhabi, UAE  
  
19 October, Scotland vs Papua New Guinea, Oman  
19 October, Oman vs Bangladesh, Oman  
  
20 October, Namibia vs Netherlands, Abu Dhabi, UAE  
20 October, Sri Lanka vs Ireland, Abu Dhabi, UAE  
  
21 October, Bangladesh vs Papua New Guinea, Oman  
21 October, Oman vs Scotland, Oman  
  
22 October, Namibia vs Ireland, Sharjah, UAE  
22 October, Sri Lanka vs Netherlands, Sharjah, UAE

Top 2 teams of each of the 2 groups of Round 1 will advance to the Super 12s.
