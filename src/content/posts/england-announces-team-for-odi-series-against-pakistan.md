---
title: "England announces team for ODI series against Pakistan"
date: 2021-07-06
categories: 
  - "cricket"
---

After announcing the Covid-19 positive of 7 Members of England Men's Cricket Team earlier today, the ECB has also announced their squad for the upcoming ODI series against Pakistan.

The team is as follows :

Ben Stokes (Captain)  
Jake Ball  
Danny Briggs  
Brydon Carse  
Zak Crawley  
Ben Duckett  
Lewis Gregory  
Tom Helm  
Will Jacks  
Dan Lawrence  
Saqib Mahmood  
Dawid Malan  
Craig Overton  
Matt Parkinson  
David Payne  
Phil Salt  
John Simpson  
James Vince  

Ben Stokes will be captaining the England's ODI side for the first time.

The PCB is satisfied by the assurances provided by the ECB and feels comfortable with protocols set for players to remain safe during the series, which will start from 8th of July in Cardiff.
