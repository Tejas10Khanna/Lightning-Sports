---

On June 17th , Spanish Tennis player Rafael Nadal tweeted about his decision of not participating in the Wimbledon and the Olympics.

After losing to Novak Djokovic in the Semi Final of the Roland Garros all his fans were ready to see him in the Wimbledon and the Olympics. But Fitness is the most important thing for a sportsman, which is why we will not be able to see him playing for quite a some time.

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet"><p lang="en" dir="ltr">Hi all, I have decided not to participate at this year’s Championships at Wimbledon and the Olympic Games in Tokyo. It’s never an easy decision to take but after listening to my body and discuss it with my team I understand that it is the right decision</p>— Rafa Nadal (@RafaelNadal) <a href="https://twitter.com/RafaelNadal/status/1405488173493456898?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw">June 17, 2021</a></blockquote>[https://platform.twitter.com/widgets.js](https://platform.twitter.com/widgets.js)

He says "Sport prevention of any kind of excess in my body is a very important factor at this stage of my career in order to try to keep fighting for the highest level of competition and titles."

He tells that it has been a team decision due to less time period between the Roland Garros and the Wimbledon,which can effect his health and fitness.

He also writes a special message for his fans in United Kingdom and Japan.

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet"><p lang="en" dir="ltr">I want to send a special message to my fans around the world, to those in the United Kingdom and Japan in particular.</p>— Rafa Nadal (@RafaelNadal) <a href="https://twitter.com/RafaelNadal/status/1405488179197747211?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw">June 17, 2021</a></blockquote>[https://platform.twitter.com/widgets.js](https://platform.twitter.com/widgets.js)
