title: "Argentina wins the Copa-America 2021!"
date: 2021-07-11
categories: 
  - "copa-america-2021"
  - "football"
---

A few minutes ago, Argentina won the Copa-America 2021 final against Brazil by 1-0.

Following the 21' minute goal from D. María, Argentina mentioned their lead against Brazil in the Semifinals. Brazil created many chances but failed to score in any of them.

It is the first major title win for Lionel Messi while playing for Argentina. Before this he had reached three Copa-America finals and a World Cup final, but lost all of them.
