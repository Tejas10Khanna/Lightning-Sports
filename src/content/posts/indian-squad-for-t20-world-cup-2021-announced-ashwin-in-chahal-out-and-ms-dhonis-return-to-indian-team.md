On Wednesday, 9PM, BCCI announced the Indian Squad for the T20 World Cup. The squad has handed out many surprises to all the Indian Fans.

The T20 World Cup is scheduled to start from 17th of October in UAE and Oman, Read about it: [ICC confirms that the ICC Twenty20 World Cup 2021 to be shifted to UAE and Oman](https://lightningsportsin.wordpress.com/2021/06/29/icc-confirms-that-the-icc-twenty20-world-cup-2021-to-be-shifted-to-uae-and-oman/)

ICC previously announced the fixtures for the T20 World Cup: [ICC T20 World Cup fixtures announced! Read More:](https://lightningsportsin.wordpress.com/2021/08/17/icc-t20-world-cup-fixtures-announced-read-more/)

**STANDBY PLAYERS**  
Shreyas Iyer  
**'Lord'** Shardul Thakur  
Deepak Chahar

The biggest announcement for Indian fans was the inclusion of **MS Dhoni** as a _mentor for the Indian side_ in the T20 World Cup.

Secretary Of BCCI Mr. Jay Shah announced the inclusion of MS Dhoni to the side.

BCCI President, Saurav Ganguly said : _"MS Dhoni's addition to the side is a way to use his experience for the T20 World Cup. I also thank Dhoni for accepting BCCI's offer to help the team for this tournament."_
