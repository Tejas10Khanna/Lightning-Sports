Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB), Punjab Kings (PBKS), Rajasthan Royals (RR), Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) have announced the replacements in their squads for the remainder of the season.

Royal Challengers Bangalore have brought Sri Lanka’s Wanindu Hasaranga as replacement for Australia’s Adam Zampa. Hasaranga picked total 7 wickets in three T20I's against India earlier this year. RCB have also bought Dushmantha Chameera for Daniel Sams, left-arm pacer George Garton for Kane Richardson and Tim David for Finn Allen.

Rajasthan Royals have brought in wicketkeeper-batsman Glenn Phillips as a replacement for England’s Jofra Archer. Rajasthan Royals have also picked South Africa’s Tabraiz Shamsi to replace Andrew Tye.

Punjab Kings have brought Australian fast bowler Nathan Ellis for Riley Meredith. Ellis picked a hat-trick against Bangladesh earlier this month. Punjab Kings have also brought English leg-spinner Adil Rashid to replace Jhye Richardson. 

KKR have replaced Australian Pat Cummins by Veteran New Zealand Pacer Tim Southee.
