The Sources have confirmed that India's Wicket-Keeper Batsman Rishabh Pant have been tested Covid-19 Positive and is home isolated from past 8-9 days.

Rishabh Pant won't be traveling with the team to Durham on Thursday.

Team India is set to play a five match test series against England, first test starting from 4th of August. The Indian players chosen for England tour currently are in a break in England.

Rishabh Pant's Covid can be very dangerous for Team India, he has scored a century while playing in England in 2018. His presence will be og great importance for India.
