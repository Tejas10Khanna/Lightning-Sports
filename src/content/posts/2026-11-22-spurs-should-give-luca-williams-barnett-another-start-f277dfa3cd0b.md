Luca Williams-Barnett scored the opener and set up Dominic Solanke in Tottenham’s 2–0 win over Ipswich Town on 21 November. The 18-year-old made both contributions on his first Premier League start. Roberto De Zerbi should give him another one against Sunderland.

**Analysis.** The two goals showed why he deserves the chance. For the first, Williams-Barnett got on the end of Pedro Porro’s deep cross. For the second, he drove down the left and sent a low ball across for Solanke to finish. Spurs had a winger who could arrive in the box when the attack developed on the opposite side and create a chance when it came through him. That gives defenders two decisions to make. Step out to meet him and he can play the ball across you. Lose track of him when the ball goes wide on the other side and he can get a shot away himself.

**Counterargument.** Ipswich’s defensive record puts the performance in context. They have gone 30 straight Premier League games without a clean sheet. A second start would give De Zerbi a chance to see how Williams-Barnett handles a different opponent with a full match of footage to study. Can he still get into the box when a full-back keeps closer tabs on him? Can he help Spurs advance the ball when there’s less room to run down the left? Those answers would tell the manager more about where the teenager fits than another look at his goal.

**Analysis.** Spurs should judge his next appearance on the quality of those decisions. Another goal would be welcome. Making the right run three times and receiving the ball once can still be a good afternoon for a winger. With Sunderland and Fulham next in the league, De Zerbi has a chance to see which parts of the Ipswich performance Williams-Barnett can repeat. Start him against Sunderland and let that performance inform the next selection.
