The All-India Women’s Selection Committee of BCCI , on Wednesday, named the Indian Women's Squad for the ODI World Cup 2022 starting from 4 March, 2022 and a 5-match ODI series and a One-off T20I match against New Zealand starting from 11 February, 2022.

**Team India squad for ICC Women's World Cup 2022 & New Zealand ODIs**:  
  
Mithali Raj _(Captain)_  
Harmanpreet Kaur _(Vice-Captain)_ Smriti Mandhana  
Shafali Verma

Exclusion of players like Jemimah Rodrigues, Shikha Pandey and Poonam Raut has surprised experts and the followers of the game.

**Punam Raut took to Twitter to express her disappointment on not being selected in World Cup. She wrote** : "_Having being considered amongst the experienced batsmen and a consistent run scorer for India, I am extremely disappointed at not being a part of the World Cup Squad. In 2021, I averaged 73.75, scoring 295 runs which included a hundred and 2 half centuries in the six ODI games that I played. It is very disheartening to be continuously left out even after performing. Having said that I would like to extend my best wishes to all the players who will be representing India_".
