Yesterday the BCCI announced the schedule for the remaining leg of the 14th Edition of the IPL, which was earlier postponed due to Covid-19 cases in the tournament.

The remaining matches of the IPL will begin from 19th of September with Chennai Super Kings vs Mumbai Indians and the Finals are scheduled to be held on the 15th of October.
