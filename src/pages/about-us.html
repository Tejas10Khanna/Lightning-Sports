Why we exist

Sport moves fast. Your news should too.

Lightning Sports was built on a simple frustration: too many sites bury the story you actually want under ads, unrelated content, and slow load times. We wanted one place — fast, clean, and written by someone who actually watches the match.

What started with Tokyo 2020 coverage has grown into a running archive across cricket, football, tennis, and the Olympics — and it's just getting started.
