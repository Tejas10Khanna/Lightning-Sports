Passionate about sport? Write for us.

It doesn't matter which sport — cricket, football, tennis, kabaddi, chess, esports, athletics, all of it. If you follow a sport closely enough to have opinions about it, we want to help you turn that passion into published work.

Why write for us

Take your fandom to the next level

You already watch every match, argue every call, and know the squad better than the commentators. Here's what you get by putting that to work.

01

A real byline

Your name, on your stories, published for an actual audience — not another anonymous forum post.

02

Cover what you love

No assignments on sports you don't follow. Pitch and write about the one (or two) you actually care about.

03

Full editorial support

Feedback on your writing, help shaping angles, and a real editor reading your drafts — not silence into a void.

04

A growing platform

Join early and grow with the site — from tournament coverage to the weekly newsletter your writing helps fill.

05

Flexible commitment

Write around a tournament you're excited about — this isn't a daily quota, it's built around when sport actually happens.

06

A community of fans

Work alongside other contributors who'll argue the same debates you do — just with a byline attached.
